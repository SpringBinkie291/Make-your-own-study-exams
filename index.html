How question generation works

V19 uses a knowledge-bank pipeline. Raw text is cleaned, converted into structured facts (definition, lab/value, finding, treatment, complication, cause/risk, diagnostic, prevention, intervention, or explicit list), and validated before question generation. MCQ distractors must come from the same fact category and pass shape/quality checks; SATA requires a coherent explicit list; numeric questions generate same-unit numeric distractors. True/False questions are deliberately balanced between supported facts and carefully altered same-category facts. Weak prose is skipped instead of forced into a question.
